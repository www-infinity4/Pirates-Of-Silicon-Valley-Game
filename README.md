# Pirates-Of-Silicon-Valley-Game
You play Steve jobs, the year is 1984, you are developing the apple computers of the time and  go through 10 levels of 10 questions about capacitor, resistance, on/off switching, building components &amp; schematics, designing the hardware &amp; software. Year by year you release new computers and have 2026 apple phone design in stage 10.
